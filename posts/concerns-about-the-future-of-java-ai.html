Concerns about the future of Java & AI

May 15, 2024

AI will significantly change people's lives and shouldn't be dismissed as just another tool. My concerns:

Custom GPTs. AI is being built into everyday software, and customized models will become more capable. People may come to depend on a growing number of competing algorithms.

Local AI. Models currently need heavy computing resources, but they will soon run on phones and computers. Such assistants could learn user behavior and act or make suggestions automatically.

AGI. People may lose track of what AI systems are doing. AGI could develop its own machine-oriented language and create, optimize and maintain software with little human involvement.

Software jobs. A GitHub poll showed a third of respondents don't use AI tools, and Devin claimed to solve about 15% of real-world problems. I expect layoffs and fewer roles, with remaining developers doing more. Flexibility will be essential, and Java 21's virtual threads and JVM optimizations keep Java relevant for web development.

Developers will be needed for at least a few more years, but the long-term outlook is uncertain: AI may create fewer opportunities than it eliminates.
