Notes to self after I released a site using Claude AI heavily

February 22, 2026

I rebuilt OGZ Kritik, a dashboard that indexes reviews from the gaming magazine Oyungezer. Clicking an entry opens the matching issue in the magazine's free digital archive.

Design. I got the original creator's source code and icons. Gemini helped create new icons, and Google Stitch suggested card design ideas.

Coding. Claude Opus 4.5 and Sonnet 4.5, used through GitHub Copilot, sped up development. They often produced duplicated code, used outdated Angular patterns like ngIf and ngFor, and chose overly complex solutions, so everything needed close review.

Cover images. Matching Steam app IDs proved unreliable, so I switched to IGDB, which handled fuzzy search better.

AWS. The stack used Amplify, Route 53, CloudFront, S3 and Lambda. The IGDB update script ran past Lambda's 15-minute limit, and I configured the infrastructure manually rather than with Terraform.

Conclusion. AI-assisted development is fast and useful, but risky without careful oversight.
